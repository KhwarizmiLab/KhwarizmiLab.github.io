NSF Workshop on Quantum Communication and Networked Systems

Researchers in classical networking, distributed systems, quantum networking, and quantum information science came together at UMass Amherst to explore the systems architecture of future quantum networks.

From the program

Workshop highlights

Across a day and a half, the program combined research talks, panels, and breakout discussions on the challenges of building quantum networks.

01

Research across the network stack

Talks explored the quantum internet, optical networks, entanglement swapping, fidelity aware rate allocation, and communication across classical infrastructure.

02

Perspectives from both communities

Panels examined open problems in quantum networking and asked what emerging quantum networks can learn from classical networking.

03

Shared questions for what comes next

Breakout groups discussed robustness, routing and resource allocation, and services such as distributed quantum computing and sensing.
